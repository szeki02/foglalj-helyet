# Alföldi utca 6 courtyard: materials and cost per workshop

**Draft, 14 October 2026.** Prices are estimates from typical Budapest retail (OBI, Praktiker, Bauhaus, steel
merchants) and second-hand listings (Jófogás, flea markets), rounded to 1,000 HUF. **Check every price before
buying.** 1 EUR ≈ 400 HUF.

Two figures per line:
- **Min** = the cash actually needed if everything that can be reclaimed, donated or bought second-hand is.
- **New** = the same thing bought new, for comparison and for grant applications.

Rules used throughout:
1. **Reuse what the yard already has.** The brown shed (roof sheets, steel posts, OSB), the pallets, the old
   screen, the willows and the concrete slabs all go back into the project.
2. **Ask before buying.** Pallets, tools, timber offcuts, cushions, bricks and soil are routinely given away.
3. **Build fittings in the workshop** (shelves, racks, sawhorses, trestles) from offcuts instead of buying them.
4. **Borrow, don't buy, the occasional tools**: hand pallet trucks, a cordless drill or an angle grinder for the
   steel day.

## 3. Ground murals — the asphalt inside the street gate, the concrete in front of the platform

As modelled, two murals in one workshop, 39 m² of paint in all:

- **Road mural** (`build_w3_road_mural.py`): the asphalt between the brick hut and the west wall, just inside the gate
  (4.3 × 5.9 m), painted as a 3 × 4 grid of 1.4 m tiles with 60 mm joints of bare asphalt. Each tile has a ground
  colour and one shape over it: a quarter circle, a half circle, a half-square triangle, or none. The curves and
  diagonals run on from tile to tile.
- **Apron mural** (`build_w3_apron_mural.py`, the area marked on the ADDITIONS layer): the concrete apron between the
  raised platform, the ramp, the WC steps and the shelter (3.0 × 4.5 m), a 2 × 3 grid of 1.5 m tiles. Four tiles
  carry one ochre circle across their shared corner; the two by the ramp a white peak pointing up it.

The colours are terracotta, ochre, deep green, slate, charcoal and off-white. The residents design both on paper, then
the grids are chalked out full size. The apron mural goes first, as a trial of the method; at the gate the grounds are
rolled row by row from the wall, and the shapes are cut in by brush along the tape.

## 4. Window posters — the machinery house windows as an exhibition

As modelled: 21 posters (B1, A1, A2, A3) painted and stencilled by the residents, pasted onto the outside of the nine
lower windows of the machinery house that face the yard (bays 07, 08 and 10–16; bay 09 is a door). They hang in four

## Where the money could come from

- **In kind before cash:** pallets, tools, timber offcuts, cushions, bricks, compost and pallet trucks are the
  things people and firms most readily give.
- **Small grants:** district (Józsefváros) civil and community grants; national small civil grants for
  community projects; corporate volunteering days from DIY retailers, which often come with materials.
- **Phasing:** stages 3, 4 and 8 cost the least; stages 1 and 5 need the most cash, so their materials are the
  first things to ask donors for.
- **Biggest single saving still available:** stage 1's timber-frame alternative, about 150,000 HUF less than the
  steel minimum. The minimum already assumes the lean castor count for stage 5.
