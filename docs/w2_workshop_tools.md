# Workshop 2 — Workshop space: tools and equipment (low budget, hand tools)

Shared workshop in the cleared lean-to (NW corner), used by residents and coordinators.
Modelled in `rhino/Alfoldi6_courtyard_site.3dm` on `09_Workshops::w2_workshop_space` by
`scripts/build_w2_workshop.py`. **M** = modelled.

The kit is deliberately small and manual: no power tools, so no dust extraction, ear protection or power
distribution is needed, and every tool can be used by residents after a short induction.
